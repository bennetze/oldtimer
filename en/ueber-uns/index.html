About us

Mario Schrank - Founder/Owner/Managing Director

Master motor vehicle mechanicMaster automotive electricianMaster coachbuilder

Growing up in a family with extensive technical experience (his father was a mechanical and automotive engineer, his grandfather an electrical engineer), his path towards a career in automotive technology was set from an early age.

At just 17, while training as an automotive mechanic, he began looking for his first classic car. It would be a 1934 DKW F2, which he went on to restore to an excellent standard, laying the foundation for his later career in classic cars. After further specialist training and master craftsman courses in the respective trades, he soon made the leap into running his own business.

Around 40 years ago, this laid the foundation for what is now the internationally recognised classic car workshop "DIE OLDTIMERMANUFAKTUR", with a highly motivated team of expertly trained staff whose restorations achieve the highest levels of craftsmanship, precision and perfection.

What began as a profession became a calling, and the enthusiasm that accompanied it grew into the passion that drives him today.

Anton Schrank

Master automotive technician

Like father, like son: at just twelve, Anton Schrank restores his first classic vehicle alongside his father, a very rare tractor made by the Swiss brand Hürlimann.

Quote: "I am fascinated by the beautiful shapes and colours, as well as the traditional engineering of historic vehicles – and what we can achieve with them."

After completing his training as an automotive mechatronics technician as the "top graduate of his year", he immediately chooses to continue his specialist education to become a master automotive technician. At just 20, he is considered Germany’s youngest master craftsman in the automotive trade.

Today, he leads "DIE OLDTIMERMANUFAKTUR" into the next generation with the same passion as his father.

The team

The professional restoration of historic vehicles brings together a wide variety of crafts.

With a team of technically skilled specialists and accomplished craftspeople – including many master craftspeople in their trades – we tackle even the most demanding challenges every day, striving for high-quality restorations that remain faithful to the original.

Our work focuses on high-quality, authentic full restorations of entire vehicles – with every stage handled in-house – as well as partial restorations to suit clients’ wishes and the overhaul of individual vehicle components.

With this unique range of services and our extensive in-house capabilities throughout the process, we cover virtually the entire value chain of high-quality classic car restoration. Our results have earned us several international awards.

We see our work as a calling and a valuable contribution to preserving technological and historical heritage with lasting value.

In recognition of this, the local automotive trade guild certified us as a "Specialist workshop for historic vehicles", making us the first and only business in its district to receive this certification.

The name "OLDTIMERMANUFAKTUR®" is a protected trademark registered by us with both the German and European trademark and patent offices.
