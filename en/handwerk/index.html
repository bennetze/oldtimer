Our craftsmanship — everything in expert hands

Bodywork/Coachbuilding

Faithful reproductions, from individual sheet-metal panels to complete bodies.

In our extensively equipped in-house bodywork shop, our highly experienced team draws on many years of expertise to repair even body damage once thought beyond repair. We have all the standard welding and metal-forming machines and tools at our disposal. Panels in steel, aluminium, stainless steel and brass are repaired, newly made, fitted and joined so that proportions, transitions and panel gaps once again form a harmonious whole.

Our coachbuilding workshop also creates, faithfully restores and reconstructs the timber frameworks needed for the sturdy body structures of earlier decades, as well as fine veneer work.

Mechanical repairs and overhauls

Our mechanical department carries out all technical repairs and overhauls. These include axles, brakes, steering and gearboxes, as well as repairs to vehicle electrical systems, right through to making complete new wiring looms.

Engine building

Here we repair engines, ignition systems and fuel systems. We also rebuild complete engines. This work naturally requires a modern range of machinery for all drilling, turning and milling operations.

Paintwork

This is where colour comes into play.

A convincing finish begins long before the first coat of paint. Carefully built-up layers, smooth surfaces and a finely balanced colour give the surface the depth and brilliance it needs.

To ensure an authentic colour scheme, we draw on important historic mixing formulas and original colour charts from past decades. Our own paint-mixing system and state-of-the-art paint facility, combined with our team’s many years of experience, ensure flawless paintwork and brilliant surfaces.

We also apply intricate coachlines, while nitrocellulose lacquer and glazing techniques form part of our extensive repertoire.

Upholstery/Trimming

The choice of materials, their distinctive feel and skilled workmanship shape an interior’s character and harmonious appearance.

From the careful refurbishment and restoration of valuable original interiors with their historic patina to the faithful recreation of complete interiors, our trimming workshop carries out all refurbishment and upholstery work using historically correct fabrics, the finest leather and fine carpets of original quality, expertly crafted. We also make authentic convertible hoods with absolute precision, including versions with genuine horsehair padding of the kind used in early vehicles.

On request, embroidery, engraving and laser work enhance these surfaces with unique decorative details.
