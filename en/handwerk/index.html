Craftsmanship — all under one roof

Bodywork

Every line begins with a close look at the surviving original material. Panels are shaped, fitted and joined until proportions, transitions and panel gaps form a coherent whole once again.

Upholstery

Materials, texture and workmanship define an interior’s character. From the first cut to the final stitch, we create upholstery that feels entirely at home in the historic vehicle.

Paintwork

A beautiful finish begins long before the first coat of colour. Carefully built layers, even surfaces and a precisely matched shade give the bodywork depth while keeping its form in focus.

Engine building

Engine building depends on many small decisions working together. Components are inspected, machined and precisely matched until individual parts become a dependable mechanical assembly once more.
