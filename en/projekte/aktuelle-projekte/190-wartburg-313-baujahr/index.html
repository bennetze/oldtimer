Current projects

Wartburg 313 S-Sport, year 1959

This Wartburg 313 Sport is also currently receiving a professional full restoration at our workshop.

From essential bodywork, a complete mechanical overhaul and entirely new electrics to a high-quality two-tone respray, chrome restoration, hood and upholstery work, we are bringing another of the most beautiful and rare sports cars of its era back to life.

Rebuilding the chassis, including axles, steering, brakes and related components.

A bespoke multicolour respray to the client’s individual specifications.

Mechanical overhaul, rebuilding the engine, twin carburettors, gearbox and other components.

The fully refurbished axles.

Restoring and re-chroming all trim pieces and fittings.

A complete instrument overhaul and restoration of the early multicoloured marque badges.

Repairing the seat frames, chrome fittings and spring units.

Refurbishing the original Schönburg radio.

As reception on the original medium- and short-wave bands can no longer be assured, we converted the surviving original radio to modern FM reception at the client’s request, adding an AUX input for MP3 playback and contemporary sound quality.

Installing the rebuilt engine, gearbox, electrics and other systems.

We have rebuilt several of these beautiful Wartburg Sports over the decades. Here is another fine example that will undoubtedly delight future generations.

In addition to completing this outstanding Wartburg Sport restoration, we made a unique 1:18 scale model for the client, as finely crafted and carefully detailed as the original — an exclusive one-off.
