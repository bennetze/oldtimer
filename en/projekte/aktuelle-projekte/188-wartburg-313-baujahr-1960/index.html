Wartburg 313 Sport, year 1960

Another rare Wartburg 313 Sport currently undergoing a full restoration at our workshop.

Widely regarded as the “most beautiful car from the East”, the Wartburg 313 Sport received numerous awards, including international honours, in its day. Today it is a highly sought-after rarity.

Just 469 were built, largely by hand, between 1957 and 1960. This beautiful, timeless two-seat sports car also earned valuable foreign currency through exports, reaching even the USA and South Africa.

This Wartburg 313 Sport somehow survived the passage of time, but its now dilapidated condition requires a complete restoration.

We have rebuilt several beautiful Wartburg 313 Sports over the years, as our references show, but the disastrous condition of this example exceeded even our expectations.

We were appalled by the failed repair and restoration attempts by previous workshops, whose work had left the car badly compromised and almost beyond recovery.

Very little of the bodywork can be saved. As part of a professional restoration, we now have to rebuild large areas section by section.

The rarity and desirability of the Wartburg 313 Sport justify the immense effort needed to restore this car faithfully and authentically to the client’s wishes.

Following the extensive bodywork, our paint shop is giving the car a high-quality, period-correct finish that also reflects the client’s individual preferences.

Alongside the paintwork, our mechanical department is comprehensively overhauling all mechanical and electrical systems.

Unfortunately, these systems appear to have been run to the point of complete wear under the previous owner and were barely salvageable.

The rare 12-stud engine, whose crankshaft main bearings were completely destroyed.

…the rebuilt engine, with entirely renewed internals, now ready to install.

Refurbishing and re-chroming all trim pieces and fittings.
