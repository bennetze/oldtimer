NSU-Fiat "Weinsberg" Cabriolet, year 1935

A stylish pre-war convertible from a marque now less familiar: a 1935 NSU-Fiat with cabriolet coachwork by Weinsberg.

Historical background:

In the late 1920s, NSU built a brand-new factory in Heilbronn. Further acquisitions and investments, however, overstretched the company financially and soon led to severe difficulties. In 1928, it had to sell the new factory, including its materials and machinery, to FIAT.

To retain the established NSU name, the company adopted the combined NSU-Fiat badge and initially built various Italian Fiat models under licence for several years.

Some beautiful original photographs from the 1930s.

This rare Weinsberg cabriolet initially appeared relatively complete and attractive, but its underlying body structure proved badly deteriorated.

We therefore separated the body from the chassis and first rebuilt the chassis, axles, brakes, steering and related components independently.

Both the structural timber framework and the body panels were badly worn and deteriorated, so the body was rebuilt from the ground up.

After around 90 years, the timber skeleton had become completely unstable. We have already recreated it entirely in high-quality ash using traditional coachbuilding methods, providing a sound structural foundation for the body.

Following the timber work, the metal outer skin, floor, bulkhead and fittings were restored and reconstructed section by section.

With all timber and metal bodywork complete, the body and its components are being prepared for paint while mechanical repairs continue.
