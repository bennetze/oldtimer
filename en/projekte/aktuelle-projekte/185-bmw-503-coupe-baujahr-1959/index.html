BMW 503 Coupe, year 1959

A rare BMW 503 Coupé in as-found condition, with several missing parts, awaiting a complete rebuild.

Only 134 examples of this final-series 503 were built by hand up to 1960. Their largely handcrafted construction means no two cars are quite alike.

Engine: BMW aluminium V8, 3.2 litres, 140 PS.

Its overall condition was pitiful; a full restoration calls on all our professional skills.

Severe rust and corrosion on steel and aluminium panels.

After completely dismantling the vehicle, we first had to remove the entire aluminium outer skin.

Heavily corroded underlying structure awaiting reconstruction.

After measuring and straightening the frame, we fabricated many panels and entire body sections and welded them into place professionally.

Frame repairs and manufacture of a new floor with crossmembers.

After rebuilding the body’s underlying steel structure, we applied a special coating before reconstructing the aluminium outer skin.

Complete reconstruction of the delicate aluminium outer skin, including faithful reproductions of many body sections.

Fitting the numerous trim pieces and attachments.

…some of the many refurbished trim pieces awaiting new chrome plating.

…a complete rebuild of the original V8 engine.

Refurbished axles, brakes, steering, shock absorbers, torsion-bar suspension and other mechanical components, ready for installation.

Making an entirely new exhaust system in stainless steel.
