ASTON MARTIN DB 2/4, year 1957

A rare 1957 Aston Martin DB 2/4 Coupé.

Six-cylinder engine, 3 litres,
twin overhead camshafts, up to 165 PS.

The car was essentially complete and could be driven to a limited extent, but it had numerous paint and body defects.

Apparently damaged in an accident decades ago and subsequently repaired using questionable methods, the supposed paint defects proved to conceal severe body damage. This had repeatedly been covered with filler, sometimes centimetres thick — a far cry from professional repair, particularly when working with aluminium bodywork.

Severe body damage beneath countless layers of paint and filler.

The stripped body, finally freed from the excessive filler applied by previous repairers.

The full extent of the serious panel damage and amateur repairs now became apparent.

Fabricating and faithfully reshaping the entire rear section in aluminium, including reinforcements.

Large areas of the wings, doors, wheel housings, wheel arches and especially the entire front end also had to be newly fabricated in aluminium.

Repairing and reinforcing the bonnet’s structural tubular frame.

Reshaping the entire front end to the client’s wishes, including the contours for the grille, headlights, indicators and air intakes.

The aluminium body is now fully restored in every detail, with the trim fitted.

Our paint shop is preparing the body and all its components for an exclusive, high-quality two-tone metallic finish.
