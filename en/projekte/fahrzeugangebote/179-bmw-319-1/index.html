BMW 319/1 Sport-Roadster, year 1936

An exceptionally restored 1936 BMW 319/1 Sport Roadster from Eisenach’s celebrated pre-war BMW sports-car era, in its original colours: green and grey-green paintwork with green genuine leather.

Meticulously restored to original specification from an excellent original basis. With all original components and numbers — chassis, body and component numbers, identification plates and more — an exceptionally rare find.

Original 1.9-litre BMW 319 engine, original triple SOLEX carburettors, centre-lock wheels, four-speed Hurth gearbox and Petri steering wheel with chrome spokes.

Predating even the BMW 328, the 319/1 Sport Roadster is considered the first thoroughbred sports car with the famous BMW twin-kidney grille.

Only 178 of these elegant, beautifully shaped roadsters were built at BMW’s Eisenach plant.
This makes the model one of the rarest cars of all.

Fewer than ten examples are thought to survive in this exceptional, fully restored and entirely matching-numbers condition.

Restored to an exceptional standard with complete attention to original detail.

Winner of several concours awards, including “Best Restored Vehicle” and “Best of Show”.

BMW certificate of authenticity, DEKRA full inspection report and German historic-vehicle assessment.
