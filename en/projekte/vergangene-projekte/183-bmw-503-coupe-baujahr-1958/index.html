BMW 503 Coupe, year 1958

A BMW 503 Coupé with the superb aluminium V8 producing 140 PS.

An extraordinary car: only 134 of this specification were built by hand in aluminium up to 1960.

We painstakingly restored many details of the car.

Steel and aluminium bodywork, including fabrication of numerous replacement panels.

A high-quality respray in the original silver metallic. Restoration or reproduction of both bumpers and various trim pieces.

Restoring the original genuine leather interior and making new carpets.

One of the very few 503 Coupés delivered with the optional factory sunroof.

With the restoration complete, one of the few surviving BMW 503 Coupés shines in all its beauty once again.
