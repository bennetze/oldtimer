Completed projects/References

BMW 327/28 Sport Cabriolet, year 1939

This BMW 327/28 Sport Cabriolet also received an extensive full restoration at our workshop.

We rebuilt it from the chassis upwards, making the complete load-bearing timber framework anew in our coachbuilding shop.

After extensive reconstruction of the entire body, we applied an elaborate, authentic blue-and-cream finish. Our upholstery shop recreated the whole interior from original references, including entirely new seats and spring units to replace the missing originals. We also made a new convertible hood with headlining and horsehair padding.

The engine overhaul used a newly manufactured cylinder head. Axles, gearbox and all mechanical components were extensively refurbished. The complete instruments, original Telefunken radio and tool kit were also recreated.

After months of detailed work, finally complete. Pictures that speak for themselves…

More than seventy years after its first delivery, the car returned to its birthplace.

After several years of demanding restoration, a faithfully detailed BMW 327/28 Sport Cabriolet is ready for delivery.

A highly successful restoration of a historically valuable vehicle, recognised as historic cultural heritage.

Before the magnificent historic backdrop of the Wartburg, in its birthplace of Eisenach.
