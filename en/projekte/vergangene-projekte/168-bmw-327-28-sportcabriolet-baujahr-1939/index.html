BMW 327/28 Sport Cabriolet, year 1939

Another BMW 327/28 Sport Cabriolet completely rebuilt at our workshop.

We knew the car well, having already restored it faithfully and to an exceptional standard around twelve years earlier.

Unfortunately, the car suffered severe damage in a rear-end collision.

We again had to straighten its partly deformed body and renew many sections, including extensive timber and panel work.

Numerous destroyed fittings and trim pieces were partly remade and fitted before our paint shop refinished and coachlined the car in its original colours.

Our upholstery shop also made a new hood with genuine horsehair padding and headlining, complete carpets and red genuine leather upholstery with contrasting piping.

Following an extensive mechanical overhaul, we returned this beautiful, faithfully restored BMW 327/28 Sport Cabriolet to its proud owner.

…an elegant and rare single-colour beauty with delicate red coachlines and red genuine leather upholstery, exactly as delivered in 1938.
