BMW 328 Sport-Roadster, year 1938

A 1938 BMW 328 Sport Roadster.

The 328 is one of the most successful and sought-after pre-war sports cars. Combining the engineering and classic design attributes of an open roadster, it won dozens of races, including the Eifelrennen, Mille Miglia and numerous hill climbs.

We have faithfully and authentically restored several outstanding BMW 328s for enthusiastic clients

(see references).

This original example retained sound underlying material, but earlier restoration and preservation attempts showed little specialist knowledge, mechanical understanding or the craftsmanship required by such a rare and exclusive pre-war roadster.

Previous bodywork largely consisted of extensive filler application. The deformed rear section, for example, had been sculpted with layers up to 4 cm thick — unrelated to proper body repair, especially lightweight aluminium construction.

For the new owner, a longstanding collector, expert and motoring enthusiast, we largely remade the entire body, faithfully restoring its curves and fit in aluminium.

All trim pieces and fittings were also refurbished and precisely refitted.

After completing the detailed bodywork, our in-house paint shop returned the car to its original delivery colour scheme.

The beautifully painted body from our paint shop.

The original engine block and the distinctive, innovative 328 cylinder head with two overhead rocker shafts, before and after the engine overhaul.

Engine: six cylinders, 2 litres, 80 PS, two overhead rocker shafts operating the V-arranged valves, triple SOLEX carburettors.

After rebuilding the engine, our mechanical department restored and installed all remaining mechanical components.

Finally, our upholstery shop faithfully recreated the complete red genuine leather interior, roadster hood, carpets and related fittings.

A beautifully and authentically restored original example of the rare BMW 328 Roadster.

Once again a thoroughbred pre-war sports roadster to drive, and a delight to admire…
