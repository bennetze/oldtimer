EMW 340 Saloon, year 1952

We comprehensively restored this EMW 340 saloon, built at Eisenacher Motorenwerke, to the client’s wishes.

Its worn condition required us to separate the body, normally welded to the chassis, so both could be rebuilt independently.

At the client’s request, we converted the roof to the sought-after folding sliding version.

Our mechanical workshop extensively overhauled the engine, gearbox, axles, dampers and all other systems.

After elaborate two-tone paintwork to the client’s wishes, our technical department reassembled the car…

…while our upholstery shop faithfully recreated the interior and rebuilt the folding sliding roof.

We completed the car with all refurbished mechanical systems, then carried out extensive testing and trial runs.

Following this outstanding restoration, it is surely among the finest restored EMW 340s.

Together with its proud owners, we are delighted to preserve this rare and precious piece of Eisenach automotive history for future generations.

Superb!
