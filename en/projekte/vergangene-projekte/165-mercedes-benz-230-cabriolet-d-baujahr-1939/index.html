Mercedes-Benz 230, Cabriolet D, year 1939

Mercedes-Benz 230, four-door Cabriolet D.

This 1939 Type 230 (W143) has a special body on an extended 3,050 mm chassis, built in small numbers at the former Mercedes-Benz factory in Mannheim — here in the exceptionally rare and luxurious

four-door convertible version, the Cabriolet D.

It underwent a complete rebuild, including the structurally important timber frame, bodywork, the customer's chosen two-tone paint finish, all mechanical components, chrome, electrics, upholstery and other fittings.

The completed and painted body.

The original engine, complete but with substantial existing damage.

A complete engine rebuild down to the smallest detail was therefore required.

Straight-six, side-valve,

2.3 litres, 55 PS,

upper-middle-class performance figures in its day.

Completely new upholstery in fine green genuine leather, harmonising with the two exterior colours.
Newly made, precisely fitted seat spring units.

An authentically made convertible hood with dressed genuine horsehair insulation and fine cotton headlining.

We also professionally restored and preserved the almost unique original fitted luggage set.

The result of this full restoration:

a dream pre-war Mercedes 230 in the exceptionally rare long, four-door Cabriolet D specification.
