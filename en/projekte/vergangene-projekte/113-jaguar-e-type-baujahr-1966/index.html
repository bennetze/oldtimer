JAGUAR E-Type S1, year 1966

A 1966 Jaguar E-Type Series 1 Convertible.
We comprehensively rebuilt the car for its owner.

Numerous areas of sometimes severe corrosion and an earlier, poorly repaired front-end accident required us to restore the entire body professionally. Our paint shop then repainted it completely in its original colour.

All chrome trim and fittings were re-plated, while the complete mechanical systems were extensively overhauled and fitted to the car.

Our upholstery shop created an entirely new interior and convertible hood, after which the finishing department subjected the car to extensive tests.
