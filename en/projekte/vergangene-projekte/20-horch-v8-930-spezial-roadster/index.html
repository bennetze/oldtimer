HORCH V8 930, Special Roadster, 1938


The winner!


First place at the

European Concours d’Elegance!

A special highlight: this Horch Special Roadster was built in 1938 to a customer's individual order by the renowned Dresden coachbuilder Gläser, as one of only seven Special Roadsters ever made.

Of seven cars built, only three survived the war. This restored example is the sole survivor with a flat windscreen and this elegantly shaped rear body.

We restored the car to the client’s wishes.

A highly successful full restoration across every trade of a historically fascinating one-off.

Winner at the European Concours d’Elegance in the grounds of Schwetzingen’s Baroque palace, awarded best-restored car in its class.

The wheels were completely rebuilt during the restoration, with specially manufactured chrome spokes — seventy per wheel.

…this Horch Special Roadster looks magnificent with the hood up too.

After our demanding restoration work was complete, this splendid car joined other celebrated classics as a crowd favourite at the renowned Sachsen Classic.

After 72 years, the car returned to its birthplace in Zwickau and the region of its makers for the first time.

We are also deeply proud of its

first place at the European Concours d’Elegance.

A tremendous honour.

It reflects the recognition of our high-quality restoration work — an occasion to celebrate together with the owner.
