DKW F12 Roadster

A 1964 Auto Union DKW F12 Roadster.

Built by Auto Union in Ingolstadt with coachwork by Baur in Stuttgart, this convertible roadster was available only in 1964. With few examples produced, it is a sought-after rarity today.

Based on Saxon pre-war Auto Union engineering, like the pre-war F9 from Zwickau, it has a proven three-cylinder two-stroke engine of 900 cc producing 45 PS. We carried out extensive repairs to its mechanical systems.
