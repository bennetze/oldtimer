Wartburg 312 Camping, year 1966

A discovery from the early GDR era: a now-rare Wartburg 312 Camping. Built in 1966 in nearby Eisenach, it received a second life here at Die Oldtimermanufaktur.

Unfortunately, it arrived in dilapidated, partly dismantled condition, with many parts lost over the years.

Despite the poor starting point, preparation allowed extensive bodywork to begin. We separated body and frame, fitted special bracing and rebuilt each independently. Our experience and stock of original parts proved invaluable.

Alongside many other repairs, we made entirely new structural sills and parts of the floor.

The frame, axles, drives, springs, brakes and steering were completely refurbished, and the remaining mechanical systems repaired.

We even had to reshape heavily rusted frame crossmembers and weld them professionally into place.

The original folding sliding roof was missing, so we had to recreate the entire assembly, including bows, lock carrier and guide rails.

During the bodywork, we also recreated and fitted the original curved trim strips along the flanks.

A completely rebuilt body, ready for paintwork.

The rebuilt frame and its mechanical attachments also received a special coating.

Paint preparation and professional treatment of the underside and wheel housings.

High-quality two-tone paint in the original colours, as requested by the client.

The restored folding-roof components and freshly chrome-plated fittings.

Following the carefully coordinated full respray, we restored the interior, electrics, glazing and remaining mechanical systems…
