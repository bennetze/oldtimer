EMW 340-7 "Estate and Courier Vehicle" year 1953

The winner!
Second place at the European Concours de Elegance!


Another Eisenach rarity comprehensively restored at our workshop: a very rare
EMW 340-7 “Estate and Courier Vehicle”.

Most people know the BMW/EMW 340 as a comfortable touring saloon, the 340-2. Several beautifully restored examples have already left our workshop; see our references.

Very few genuine 340-7 estate and courier vehicles were built between 1953 and 1955 — archives record just 364. Fewer than five are known today.
Given its rarity, we restored the car faithfully and to the highest standard for a valued client, classic-car enthusiast and collector.

Built in Eisenach and delivered to Finland, the car had been
kept running there over the years.
However, the necessary repairs and maintenance proved to have been carried out with little expertise.


We had to repair substantial body damage and fabricate accurately fitting replacement panels.

Making new axle pins and bronze bushes.

Axle,

brake and gearbox overhaul.


In addition to making a faithful new radiator, we had entirely new cast brake drums manufactured for the car.

During the body repairs, we also partly renewed the structurally important load-bearing timber components.

At the client’s request, our paint shop applied an elaborate, authentic period two-tone finish.

Our paint specialists also professionally restored the numerous real-wood trim pieces, including the elegant woodgrain finish.

Alongside the complete mechanical overhaul, our upholstery shop renewed the entire interior: a headlining tailored from fine cotton cloth, beige genuine leather with blue-grey piping, real linoleum for the load floor and natural-fibre carpets.

Following the successful restoration of this rare automotive treasure, we and our customer were asked to lend the almost unique, immaculate 340-7 estate to the museum in Eisenach so that a wider public could enjoy it. The car subsequently became a special exhibit at the AWE ‘Automobile Welt Eisenach’ museum through this friendly loan.

At the foot of the Wartburg, where the car was originally built, a special exhibition lasting several months celebrated 70 years since the development of the 340. The rarity of this 340-7 estate and the quality of our restoration were recognised there too. Delivered from this very place in 1953, it returned to its birthplace for the first time after more than 66 years.

Another highlight for this vehicle:
Every year, the Classic Gala Schwetzingen and its European Concours de Elegance recognise outstanding classic-car restorations from around the world.


This superbly restored example attended by special invitation and received one of the coveted trophies from the expert jury:
(Second place from the Classic Jury.), bringing praise and recognition for our restoration work.


The first prize, incidentally, also went to a car restored at our workshop:
a BMW 328 Vignale Roadster from 1938
(see our references).


We are proud that both the owner — a longstanding client, expert and collector of these vehicles — and an international jury recognise the outstanding quality of our work.
