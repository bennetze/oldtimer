BMW 326 Touring Cabriolet "Autenrieth", year 1938

A rare BMW 326 touring cabriolet built in 1938 in Eisenach, Thuringia, with Autenrieth coachwork from Darmstadt.

Preserved in a Swiss family for around fifty years, the cabriolet was completely and faithfully rebuilt at our workshop for its owners.

Although largely complete, it was heavily worn overall and partly altered from original specification.

Extensive metal and timber bodywork, authentic paint, mechanical repairs, new chrome, a complete new wiring loom, interior restoration and a newly made convertible hood called on virtually all our restoration trades.

The fully restored body provides an ideal foundation for an excellent paint finish.

Paint preparation, checking all surfaces and panel fit.

Our paint shop then began the high-quality two-tone finish, including intricate coachlines.

We also restored and preserved the original solid-wood interior trim.

In parallel, our mechanical and precision-engineering departments refurbished the axles, brakes, steering, torsion-bar suspension, dampers, electrics and instruments, preparing them for installation.

We made accurately fitting timber bows for the hood, then tailored a new outer hood and headlining with authentic horsehair padding.

The entire interior was renewed in fine blue genuine leather with contrasting blue piping as originally fitted, and new blue natural-fibre bouclé carpets were made and fitted.

With every component assembled, the car shines in its original beauty once more. It made a final visit to Eisenach, where it was born 85 years earlier, before its journey back to its delighted owners in Switzerland.

…the work is complete.

A faithfully and authentically restored BMW 326 Autenrieth Cabriolet.
