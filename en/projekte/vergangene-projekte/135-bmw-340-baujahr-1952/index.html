BMW 340, year 1952

This early BMW 340 is still a genuine BMW, built by Bayerische Motorenwerke in Eisenach, where until the
Second World War and for a few years afterwards every BMW car was manufactured.

From the Dixi models of the 1910s and 1920s through the BMW 303, 309, 315, 319, 320, 321, 326, 327, 327/28, 328, 329 and 335 of the 1930s and 1940s, to the early post-war BMW 340 developed from the proven 326, all these cars came from Eisenach.

This imposing BMW 340 saloon with its sought-after folding sliding roof was completely and faithfully rebuilt at our workshop.

Following extensive bodywork and a client-requested cathodic dip coating of the entire body, we reinstated the rare folding sliding roof.

Our paint shop then refinished the car in its original colours.

New chrome and fully refurbished instruments.

After thoroughly overhauling all mechanical components, we had the unavailable original upholstery fabric faithfully rewoven for the complete interior rebuild.

The workmanship, originality and quality of this extensive restoration are hard to surpass.

A feast for the eyes and surely one of the finest 340s…
