Wartburg 311-2 Cabriolet, year 1957

A rare Wartburg 311 Cabriolet, built in nearby Eisenach in 1957 and delivered to northern Germany.

Until recently it remained with its first owner. We restored it in every detail for the client.

The body arrived completely dismantled and rather fragmentary.

Extensive damage required a complete body rebuild and separate chassis restoration.

Following the demanding bodywork, the car received a two-tone metallic finish based on the original 1957 export colours and the client’s preferences.

Our upholstery shop then faithfully recreated the entire interior in fine leather and made the elaborate convertible hood with genuine horsehair padding.

Well-equipped export cabriolets in particular featured genuine wood and leather interiors.

We faithfully recreated these details and restored the finish using period glazing techniques.

Once the engine, gearbox, axles, electrical system and other mechanical components were thoroughly overhauled, we carried out extensive tests and trial runs.

The completed restoration presents the car in beautiful condition.

A unique example sure to delight any collector…
