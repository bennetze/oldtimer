Completed projects/References

Wartburg 313 S - Sport, year 1959

Another Wartburg 313 Sport completely and faithfully restored at our workshop.

Despite apparently satisfactory condition, it too had suffered poor work by previous “experts”.

Even structural sills had been “preserved” by placing new patches over completely rusted metal and concealing them with generous filler.

Earlier accident damage around the structural A-pillars had been superficially patched; we repaired it properly using specially fabricated sections.

With virtually no usable replacement parts available for the rare 313 Sport, our body shop had to make and fit them individually to the car.

Step by step, we professionally repaired all the damage, some of it severe.

The completed body now has excellent surfaces and panel gaps. At the client’s request, we faithfully recreated the missing folding soft top and fitted all its brackets and crossmembers.

A useful comparison between a completed, fully restored 313 Sport and this painstakingly rebuilt body, now ready for high-quality paintwork in our paint shop.

Our paint specialists applied an exclusive, specially mixed colour to the client’s wishes.

The fully restored chrome parts and refurbished 313-specific instruments.

Once all mechanical components, including the engine, gearbox, axles and drives, had been refurbished, the freshly painted body was reunited with its restored frame. Our assembly workshop made and installed a new wiring loom and fitted all the remaining mechanical equipment.

We also comprehensively restored the rare original BERLIN radio, converted it from 6 to 12 volts and, through some technical ingenuity, adapted its medium-wave channel to modern FM reception at the customer's request. Medium-wave broadcasts had ceased at the beginning of 2016.

Our upholstery workshop then rebuilt the original seats with new spring cores and retrimmed the entire interior to the customer's wishes in fine beige leather with red contrast piping. A new convertible hood was tailored to fit.

Once every component was complete, we carried out extensive final tests before handing the car over to its proud owners, a couple we have known well for years.

Having already restored many exclusive treasures for them, we are sure that this beautifully restored WARTBURG 313 SPORT will further enrich their unique collection of classics.

Our entire team at Die Oldtimermanufaktur would like to thank V. and R. once again and wish them many happy journeys in this automotive treasure…

One of its first outings took it back to its historic birthplace, the AWE Automobil-Werk-Eisenach, and to the Wartburg castle that gave the car its name, popularly called the ‘most German’ of castles. From every angle, the car reveals stylish design details that delight any motoring enthusiast.
