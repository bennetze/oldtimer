Mercedes-Benz 190SL, year 1956

A 1956 Mercedes-Benz 190 SL — a rare and desirable example from the early production period.

Delivered to the USA, the car was kept running there for decades, although inspection revealed questionable methods and amateur repair attempts.
The result was severe corrosion throughout the vehicle.

We therefore undertook a thorough restoration to original specification at our workshop.

Even the load-bearing axle mounting areas of the body had been repaired in the supposedly “land of unlimited opportunity” using highly questionable materials such as newspaper, steel wool from pan scourers and generous quantities of plaster…

We therefore had to fabricate virtually the entire lower third of the body anew, professionally replacing it along with the floor and its structural members…

Meanwhile, our engine department comprehensively overhauled the engine and ancillaries, gearbox, complete axles, steering, brakes and related components.

At the client’s request, our paint shop also returned the car to its original 1956 delivery colour.

During the mechanical rebuild we made and installed a completely new wiring loom, among other work, and renewed the entire interior in fine leather. We used authentic materials matching the quality of those used in 1956, including chrome-free-tanned leather with the original type of surface colouring.

As part of the interior restoration, the car also regained its sought-after early bucket seats, which were completely rebuilt.

Surely one of the finest early examples of the beautiful Mercedes-Benz 190 SL Roadster.
