BMW 340, year 1951

A 1951 BMW 340 saloon.

Delivered in worn but salvageable condition, this BMW received a faithful full restoration for its owner, a BMW enthusiast.

It too had suffered questionable repairs at the hands of less-skilled previous “experts”.

Faithfully fabricating numerous panels, entire body sections and even load-bearing frame crossmembers.

Overhauling axles, springs, brakes, steering and related components.

At the client’s request, we made and fitted an authentic folding sliding roof, a rare feature even in its day.

Following the extensive bodywork, our paint shop applied a professional finish.

…a comprehensive engine rebuild and overhaul of many other mechanical components.
We then completed the mechanical assembly, made entirely new electrics and wiring, and electroplated all trim with chrome.

The faithfully recreated woodgrain finish, known as “beer glaze”, on the dashboard and decorative panels.

A BMW 340 beautifully rebuilt down to the details.

An inviting interior with the sought-after folding sliding roof.

The engine bay is now not only mechanically overhauled but visually appealing once again.

Ready for the open road…
