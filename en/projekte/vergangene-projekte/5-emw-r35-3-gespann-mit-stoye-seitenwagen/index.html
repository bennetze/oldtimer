EMW R35/3 combination with “Stoye” sidecar

This EMW R35/3 combination suffered a severe rear-end collision, damaging the motorcycle’s rear, shaft drive, gearbox and engine, as well as the Stoye sidecar.

We therefore completely rebuilt the entire combination for its owner.

Alongside straightening and bodywork on the motorcycle, the completely destroyed sidecar had to be rebuilt.

The newly made aluminium sidecar body.

Following this faithful rebuild, we returned the restored motorcycle to its proud owner. It has proved an ideal combination even on longer tours.
