AMC Pacer, year 1979

A 1979 AMC Pacer.

This American Motors Corporation car featured highly distinctive, futuristic styling and was built in comparatively small numbers. Far fewer reached Europe and Germany, so only a small surviving population is expected in Germany today, supported by a devoted following.

With innovative engineering and a unitary body incorporating side-impact protection, the Pacer was regarded as an exceptionally safe American car in its day. Compact by US standards but notably wide, its broad track and low centre of gravity gave it stable handling. It combined safety and comfort with lively road manners, and offered generous factory equipment with a broad choice of colours and upholstery.

We carried out a comprehensive restoration to the client’s wishes. During the bodywork, many panels had to be individually fabricated, fitted and welded into place.

Our paint shop then gave the car a professional full respray in its original gold metallic delivery colour.

Engine: 4.2 litres, 120 PS.

We also comprehensively overhauled the mechanical systems, which the previous owner had completely dismantled, restoring or replacing many parts before reinstalling them.

The AMC Pacer's unusual design and vast glass surfaces have made it a sought-after collector's car with the cult status of a design icon.

After all components had been overhauled and parts of the upholstery, headlining, carpets and other trim renewed in our upholstery workshop, the customer was able to take delivery of the beautifully restored AMC Pacer.
