Mercedes-Benz 300 ADENAUER, 4-door Cabriolet-D, year 1953

A Mercedes-Benz 300 Adenauer, a very rare early 1953 four-door Cabriolet D.

This Mercedes flagship entered automotive history as the Adenauer Mercedes: initially as a saloon, it was the first official car of West Germany’s first Chancellor, Konrad Adenauer. President Theodor Heuss and other international political leaders also used this comfortable prestige car, then Daimler-Benz’s leading product.

According to the available expert report, the car was originally delivered to New York. It returned to Germany in the 2000s and was comprehensively restored here at our workshop on behalf of its owner.

Six-cylinder engine with 3 litres displacement and 115 PS.

Numerous areas of body damage and corrosion required us to reconstruct the entire body using precisely fabricated replacement panels.

In a high-quality frame-off restoration, body and chassis are normally separated so every component can be rebuilt independently.


Only once body and chassis work is complete are they precisely reunited, allowing accurate body alignment and panel gaps.


We also renewed and fitted the timber bows required for the hood structure.

Our paint shop then refinished the car in an original Mercedes colour chosen by the client.

As part of this comprehensive restoration, our in-house upholstery shop tailored an entirely new fine genuine leather interior and convertible hood with real horsehair padding.

Complete instrument overhaul, including conversion from miles to kilometres and Fahrenheit to Celsius.

Full overhaul of the still-original six-cylinder engine, together with gearbox repairs.

Fine upholstery, a new convertible hood and exquisite genuine wood trim from our workshop.

The original Becker Nürburg radio.

Only 52 examples of this early 1953 Type 300(a) were built as four-door Cabriolet Ds.

One of the few Adenauer cabriolets restored professionally, faithfully and to such a high standard leaves our workshop for its owner, an expert, enthusiast and collector of rare automotive treasures.
