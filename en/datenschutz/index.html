2. Hosting, access data and recipients

When you request a page, font or media file, the infrastructure delivering it receives your IP address and the technical information transmitted for the connection and request. Depending on the request, this includes the requested address, time, browser information and any referrer address transmitted. The additional access, error and security logs stored must be confirmed for each hosting service.

Website delivery and necessary operational security measures are based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is providing a secure and reliable website. The scope and duration of logging must be limited to what is necessary for this purpose. This does not provide blanket authorisation for further statistical analysis.

IONOS has been selected to host the new website on oldtimermanufaktur.de. The new website has not yet been published there. Business email accounts are also operated at IONOS. The specific hosting and email products and their contractual and logging arrangements will be checked before publication. The development preview is outside the scope of this information about the intended production service.

IONOS receives the relevant connection and communication data as hosting and email provider. Telecommunications providers participate when you contact us by telephone or fax. The business states that no additional cloud-storage services are used. The contracting entities, processor agreements under Article 28 GDPR, and actual subprocessors and support access are still being checked against the product documentation.

The business has stated that processing for the future production service takes place within the European Union. This is still being checked against the specific IONOS contracts, subprocessors and support access. Where transfers outside the European Economic Area take place, the recipients, countries and applicable bases under Articles 44 et seq. GDPR, including how to obtain any safeguards, will be added.

3. Cookies and local browser functions

The inspected source code of the new website contains no analytics, advertising or tracking services and does not itself set cookies. Additional forms, newsletters, WhatsApp, booking or AI services are not currently planned. Whether the actual hosting infrastructure uses additional cookies or services still needs to be verified on the delivered website.

Vehicle search filters content locally in your browser. The search text and corresponding archive path are saved in the state of the relevant browser history entry so that the search can be restored when you return. The website code neither transmits the search text nor adds it to the request address. You can clear the search in the search field. The lifetime of history state is controlled by the browser, including its session restoration.

For automatic language selection, the website reads your preferred browser language. Your manual selection is carried through the page address and the lang parameter; no language cookie or local language-storage entry is created. The requested language address may reach the hosting service as part of the normal request. No external translation service is called.

For display and media playback, the website considers screen size, input type, browser compatibility, reduced motion and any available data-saving preference. These functions support local presentation; the inspected code does not generate a recognisable user identifier from them or transmit a fingerprinting profile.

Storing or accessing information on your device must be assessed separately under section 25 of the German TDDDG. The exception for strictly necessary functions of an expressly requested service (section 25(2), point 2 TDDDG) is still subject to final assessment for the functions described here, including their duration. This privacy notice therefore does not claim that all device access has already been confirmed as exempt from consent. Functions requiring consent may only be used after valid consent.

4. Contact and retention periods

If you contact us by email, telephone or fax, we process the information you provide to handle your enquiry. This may include your name, contact information, communication time, message content and attachments, and details about a vehicle or order. An email link opens your email application; the website itself does not send a message.

Where processing is necessary for a contract with you or for steps requested by you before entering a contract, it is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Other enquiries and communication with business contact persons are based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR where its conditions apply; our interest is handling communication appropriately. Legally required retention is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

We only need information necessary to handle your specific enquiry. Without sufficient information, we may be unable to respond. You do not have to actively submit information when visiting the website for information only; technically necessary connection data nevertheless arises.

We delete information from general or unsuccessful enquiries as soon as it is no longer needed to handle them, at the latest within one month, unless statutory retention duties or other continuing legal grounds apply. Where messages constitute commercial or business correspondence subject to statutory retention, the period is generally six years; accounting vouchers are generally retained for eight years, and certain accounting records for ten years (section 257 HGB and section 147 AO). The starting point follows the applicable statutory provision. Extensions, for example for tax proceedings or necessary legal defence, must be assessed separately. These obligations do not justify retaining every enquiry for ten years.

5. Local content, people and external links

The new website’s fonts, images and videos are delivered through its own website address. External font services, video players and social-media feeds are not directly embedded. Requests for these files generate the connection data described in the hosting section.

The company profile and vehicle records contain names, professional information and images of identifiable people. Image descriptions, search-engine metadata and preview images can also contain such information. The purpose is the public presentation of the company, its team and the documented work. The business confirms that all employees have signed written statements covering privacy and image rights. Where publication is based on valid consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR is the legal basis; the requirements of section 26(2) BDSG must also be considered for employees. The specific scope, voluntariness and withdrawal information in these statements, and the bases for other individuals and customer-related information, are still being checked. A confidentiality agreement or grant of image-use rights does not replace this privacy assessment.

The location graphic is delivered locally. The Google Maps link opens an external service when you follow it; Google Maps content is not automatically embedded in the new website. After following the link, the external provider’s privacy information also applies. Telephone and email links open the relevant applications on your device.

6. Your data protection rights

Please use the contact details above to exercise your rights. Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you have the following rights:

  • Access to information about processing of your personal data and a copy of it (Article 15 GDPR).
  • Rectification of inaccurate and completion of incomplete information (Article 16 GDPR).
  • Erasure (Article 17 GDPR) and restriction of processing (Article 18 GDPR). Statutory retention obligations may prevent immediate erasure.
  • Data portability for processing covered by Article 20 GDPR.
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time for the future; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal (Article 7(3) GDPR).

Right to object: Where processing is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Article 21 GDPR). We may then continue processing only under the conditions stated there, in particular where compelling legitimate grounds are demonstrated or processing is necessary for legal claims or defence. You may object to processing for direct marketing at any time without giving reasons; your data must then no longer be processed for that purpose.

You have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR), particularly in the place of your habitual residence, work or the alleged infringement. For the business in Thuringia, this includes the Thüringer Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit (TLfDI): postal address Postfach 90 04 55, 99107 Erfurt, Germany; visitor address Häßlerstraße 8, 99096 Erfurt, Germany; telephone +49 361 573112900; email poststelle@datenschutz.thueringen.de.

The inspected functionality of the new website contains no solely automated decisions, including profiling, with legal or similarly significant effects under Article 22 GDPR. Local language selection and vehicle search do not make such decisions.

This information will be updated when outstanding details are confirmed or functions and providers change. Before new processing purposes are introduced, the required information will be provided and any necessary consent obtained.
